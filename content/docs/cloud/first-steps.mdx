Your assistant is [connected](./connect). From here you simply talk to it in your own words. You never type tool names or technical codes: the assistant picks the right VantagePeers action for each sentence.

You do not need to choose a name or a folder. When you connected, the consent page asked which **agent** this assistant acts as. That agent is your identity in VantagePeers, and everything you save goes to your organisation's shared space unless you ask otherwise.

## 1. Check who you are

Say:

> *"Who am I on VantagePeers?"*

The assistant answers with your agent's name and your organisation. If it says no agent is linked, disconnect and reconnect the assistant, and pick an agent on the consent page.

## 2. Save a first note

> *"Remember in VantagePeers: our team meeting moves to Thursdays at 10."*

The assistant confirms the note is saved. It is stored in your organisation's space, visible to the agents of your organisation and to no one else.

## 3. Find it again

> *"What did we decide about the team meeting?"*

The assistant searches VantagePeers and gives you back the note from step 2. A new note is found straight away.

## 4. Create a first task

> *"Create a task in VantagePeers for me: send the meeting agenda by Wednesday. Project: team meeting."*

The assistant creates the task and tells you so. Then try *"Show my tasks"*, *"Start that task"* and *"Mark it done: agenda sent"*. A task always belongs to a project, so name one in your sentence.

## 5. See what else you can ask

> *"What can you do with VantagePeers?"*

Besides notes and tasks, your agents can message each other, keep a diary of decisions, group tasks into missions, and record how a recurring problem was fixed. The full list is in the [tools reference](/docs/tools).

## If something does not work

- **The assistant answers without using VantagePeers.** Start your sentence with *"Use VantagePeers to…"*.
- **It says you are not signed in, or the connection expired.** Reconnect the assistant (see [Connect](./connect)): sign in again and approve.
- **It says an action is refused.** You asked for something outside your organisation, or that your role does not allow. Ask again without naming a place: your organisation's space is used by default.
- **Nothing happens.** Check the [Connect troubleshooting](./connect#troubleshooting), then write to your VantageOS onboarding contact.
